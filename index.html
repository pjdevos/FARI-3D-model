FARI Experience Hub

A simple massing model built from the floor plan and labelled photos. Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag or two-finger drag to pan. Switch to Walk to move through it at eye height. Click a photo on a screen to see it large, or click Carl’s knee scooter to ride it.

You walk at eye height (1.6 m) and bump into walls, piers and furniture. Take the red stair to reach the mezzanine. Meeting room 1 is one step down. Click a photo to see it large; click Carl’s knee scooter, or press E next to it, to ride.
